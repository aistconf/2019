Intel® Distribution of OpenVINO™ toolkit: a case study of semantic segmentation

July 19, 2019 11:00 am - 12:30 pm

Evgenii Vasilyev
Gleb Gladilov

We provide a detailed overview of the Intel® Distribution of OpenVINO™ toolkit.  We describe the toolkit components (Inference Engine, Model Optimizer, OpenCV, etc.), their capabilities and applications. The practical use of the OpenVINO™ toolkit is represented on the example of semantic segmentation problem. We consider step-by-step implementation of the problem solution based on deep learning approach. The Inference Engine component is used for high-performance inference of deep models. We demonstrate synchronous and asynchronous inference modes, and implement the first one.

Hardware and software requirements

Hardware:

  • 3d generation of Intel® Core™ processors or higher

OS:

  • Ubuntu 16.04 or higher (LTS) 64-bit
  • Centos 7.4 or higher 64-bit
  • Microsoft Windows 10 64-bit

Software:

  • Python 3.*
  • Intel® Distribution of OpenVINO™ toolkit R5 or higher [https://software.intel.com/en-us/openvino-toolkit]

 

* If possible, tutorial organizers will provide an access to servers with preinstalled software. In this case, only Internet access is required.
