Deadline moved to May, 05

  1. Home

Dear friends,

We have received a good number or abstract submissions, thank you!

To leave some extra room for you to prepare with full papers, we move the full paper submittion deadline to May 05 (end of day anywhere). After that point we distribute the submissions to the academic reviewers, please be on time here.
A little Q&A on submissions compiled from questions we received recently.

Q. Can I submit an article if I did not submit abstract on time?
A. Yes. We collect abstracts for evaluation of the number of participants and to be ready with appropriate number of reviewers.

Q. Can I correct abstract or title after submission?
A. Yes, you edit your submission on EasyChair.
